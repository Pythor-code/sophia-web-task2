A Bakery for Our Neighbors

Our story

North Star Bakery grew from a love of sharing fresh bread. Our neighborhood shop gives commuters a quick morning stop and families a place to choose a treat.

How we choose ingredients

We value simple ingredients and dependable suppliers. We look for seasonal options when available and keep our menu flexible so we can bake in small batches.

Meet the team

Alex, bread baker

Alex prepares our daily loaves and enjoys helping customers choose bread for a shared meal.

Jamie, pastry and cake baker

Jamie works on the pastry selection and helps families plan cakes for birthdays and other celebrations.
